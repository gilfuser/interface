# X-Touch Mini r8 — quick reference

> **A edits · B mixes · MC connects · Record captures what you move**

## Controls shared by Layers A and B

| B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | B5 | B6 | B7 | B8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MC | Palindrome | Context | Context | Looped | Stop | Play/Pause | Record/Overdub |

- Hold B6: clear all recorded takes.
- Slider: 40–200 BPM; center = 120.

## Layer A — edit

- Top 1–8: select orbit; selected orbit blinks.
- Turn encoder 1–8: edit parameter 1–8.
- Push and turn the encoder matching the selected orbit: dry/wet.
- B3/B4: previous/next process.
- Solid top LED: selected process is active in that orbit.

## Layer B — mix

- Turn encoder 1–8: orbit level.
- Top 1–8: mute/unmute.
- Push encoder 1–8: solo/unsolo.
- B3: unmute all.
- B4: unsolo all.
- Top LED: off = inaudible, solid = audible, blink = solo.

## MC — connect

- B1 from A or B: enter MC.
- Process button: select process.
- Push encoder N: insert/remove process in orbit N.
- Turn encoder N: wet in orbit N, when available.
- Ring off: absent; full: active; proportional: wet.
- Layer A alone: exit to A.
- Layer B alone: exit to B.
- Layer A+B: toggle router/timeline button LEDs.

## Timeline view

- Top row: 8 Tidal cycles.
- Bottom row: one LED every 4 cycles; 32-cycle phrase.
- Routing controls remain active.
- Rings continue showing process routing/wet.

## Automation

1. Press B8.
2. Move any encoder in Layer A or B, across any process/orbit.
3. Press B8: the take closes and starts playing.
4. Press B8 again while playing to record another overdub.

- Four takes are available by default.
- A take may combine parameters, dry/wet and Layer B orbit levels.
- The oldest is replaced only when the pool is full.
- B7 pauses/resumes or restarts all takes.
- B6 stops but preserves takes.
- Hold B6 to clear all takes.
- Moving an automated route takes it over manually.
